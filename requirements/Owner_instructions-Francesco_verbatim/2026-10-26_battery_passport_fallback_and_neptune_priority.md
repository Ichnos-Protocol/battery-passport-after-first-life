# 2026-10-26: the battery passport in CX-Titan, the fall-back, and CX-Neptune deprioritized

Francesco's words, verbatim, in his message of 26 October 2026 that answered decisions Q-985, Q-986 and Q-987 and the
summary of the release check.

> [The first part of the message, on three decisions of the battery passport application, is left out of this extract.]
>
> Keep the Tests by Neptune. The point is that Neptune is not fully release, so we need to become 100% compliant with Titan, and become compliant with Neptune later, so do not regress Neptune code, but deprioritize it only.
