# 2026-11-11: the manufacturer identity of a passport

## Francesco's words

Francesco's words, verbatim, in his message that answered the explanation of the three operational points of the step 8
plan:

> 1. this behavior is wrong: have we not derived from the requirement that the original manufacturer is always traceable as passports for reused or repurposed batteries carry links to it? And the remanufacturer or repurposers who issue the new passport must be permanent. Is overwriting the manufacturers identification fields explicitly prohibited and guarded against?  Recheck with the requirements and the texts, and if they confirm, the application behavior must enforce this by design and guard against changing.
> [The rest of the message, on the demo deployment of the battery passport application, is left out of this extract.]

The words set one rule for the work of this repository. The manufacturer identification of a passport is permanent: the
original manufacturer on the original passport, and the second-life operator (remanufacturer, repurposer, preparer for
re-use) on the new passport. The application enforces the rule by design and guards against any change.
