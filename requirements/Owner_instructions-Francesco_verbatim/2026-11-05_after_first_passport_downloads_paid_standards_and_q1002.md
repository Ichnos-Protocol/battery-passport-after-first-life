# 2026-11-05: the step checkpoints of the after-first-passport work and decision Q-1002

Francesco's words, verbatim, in two messages that answered the step 1 checkpoint of the after-first-passport work
(the Digital Product Passport Expert Group task on CX-0160 Battery Passport):

> [One line of the message, on the downloads of source texts, is left out of this extract.]
> regarding Decision Q-1002 I have not understood what options are there

> Option A: the company which prepares the battery will also be the one which in interested in buying the battery. The previous owner who wants to sell the battery is the data owner, so according to GDPR he must consent to the data usage, so I understand the intent of the legislator

The words answer one point of the checkpoint on the work of this repository: decision Q-1002 (who reads the state of
health and battery status under the draft Article 77(9) act) takes option A. The application follows draft Article 1(4)
once the Commission adopts the act, and no feedback is sent. Francesco's reason: the operator that prepares a battery
for re-use, repurposing or remanufacturing is the prospective purchaser of the battery, and the owner who sells the
battery must consent to the use of the battery's data.

## The step 2 checkpoint

Francesco's words, verbatim, in his message that answered the step 2 checkpoint (the citation catalogue), whose question asked whether he accepts the four recommendations of decisions Q-1007 to Q-1010:

> Accept all four

The words accept the recommendation of each decision: Q-1007 (which producer takes over the passport of a second-life
battery that becomes waste), Q-1008 (whether the original passport records a link to its successor), Q-1009 (whether a
new passport is a new registration in the EU registry) and Q-1010 (who authorises the wider sharing of a second-life
battery's data). The words do not answer the three downloads that the step 2 checkpoint also listed.

## The SYS.1 review

Francesco's words, verbatim, in his message that answered the review of the SYS.1 stakeholder requirements of step 3
(18 new and 4 changed entries, uncommitted):

> the purchaser may share with prospective purchasers? are you sure it is not the seller who shares? double check
> the first recycler collects and reports the recycling information of every facility, and upstream operators supply data to the first recycler? are you sure? who are these recyclers? does the recycler not report to the manufacturer? who are other recyclers upstream? recycler is a clearly defined role who converts a battery into raw materials, there cannot be a chain of many recyclers, unless they break down the process in preparation a pyro process and a hydro process. double check those requirements. the others are approved

The words ask to recheck StR-PASS-969 (the purchaser's share with prospective purchasers), StR-LIFE-961 and
StR-LIFE-962 (the first recycler collects and reports the information of several facilities) and StR-LIFE-963 (the
operators upstream from the first recycler provide data), and approve the other entries of the SYS.1 set.

## The use cases of the reference implementation

Francesco's words, verbatim, in his message that answered the corrected wording of StR-PASS-969, StR-LIFE-961,
StR-LIFE-962 and StR-LIFE-963:

> my reference implementation in Catena-X must be able to share this data with the issuer of the passport, if a contract exist, so the purchasing of this data can be automated via Catena-X. This must be registered as a Use case of the reference implementation. Other use cases must be recoreded, such as the sharing for reselling, the lifecycle status from waste status and without passing from wate and the direct reuse of a battery without preparation, so without a new passport are all use cases to be documented in the CX standard amended and as SYS.2 requirements.
>
> the rest is approved

The words approve the corrected wording of the four entries and name four use cases of the reference implementation,
each to be documented in the CX-0160 amendment proposal and as SYS.2 system requirements:

1. The sharing of the individual battery information of Annex XIII point 4 with the issuer of the passport, when a
   contract exists, so that the purchase of the data runs automatically through Catena-X.
2. The sharing of the same information for a resale (draft Art. 2(4)(c) of the Art. 77(9) act).
3. The lifecycle status change of a battery that becomes waste and is then prepared for re-use or for repurposing, and
   the lifecycle status change of a battery that is repurposed or remanufactured without becoming waste.
4. The direct re-use of a battery without preparation, which keeps the passport (no new passport).
